Section 04 Academic & Community Impact

Significance

SIGNIFICANCE OF THE ASSIGNED TOPIC

This study aims to understand how technology use affects the academic performance of first-year Collmputer Engineering students at Batangas State University – Alangilan Campus. The findings may provide valuable insights into the benefits and challenges of technology in education and may benefit the following individuals and groups:

Stakeholder Scope hub
05 Beneficiary Groups

Spanning learners, faculty, institutional leadership, families, and future scholars.

school
01 / Learners

Students.

This study may benefit first-year Computer Engineering students by helping them understand how their use of technology affects their academic performance. It may encourage them to develop responsible and effective technology habits, such as using digital tools for research, studying, communication, and completing academic tasks. Furthermore, it may help them recognize potential distractions, including excessive social media use and non-academic activities, that could negatively affect their learning and productivity.

co_present
02 / Faculty

Teachers and Educators.

The findings of this study may help teachers and educators understand how technology influences students' learning habits, classroom participation, and academic performance. Through the results, they may identify effective ways to integrate digital tools and educational platforms into their teaching strategies. It may also help them develop appropriate approaches to guide students in using technology productively while minimizing its possible negative effects on learning.

account_balance
03 / Institution

Batangas State University – Alangilan Campus.

This study may provide the university with valuable information regarding the role of technology in the academic experiences of first-year Computer Engineering students. The findings may serve as a basis for improving educational strategies, strengthening digital learning resources, and developing programs that promote responsible and effective technology use. Additionally, it may help the institution identify areas where improvements in technological facilities and academic support are needed.

family_restroom
04 / Home Support

Parents and Guardians.

This study may help parents and guardians become more aware of how technology affects students' academic performance. By understanding its positive and negative influences, they may provide better guidance and support in developing healthy technology habits at home. It may also encourage them to create an environment that promotes academic responsibility, proper time management, and balanced technology use.

biotech
05 / Scholarship

Future Researchers.

This study may serve as a reference for future researchers who wish to explore the relationship between technology use and academic performance among college students. It may provide relevant information and insights that can support further studies involving other academic programs, year levels, or educational institutions. Moreover, it may encourage additional research on the challenges and opportunities brought by technological advancements in Philippine higher education.
